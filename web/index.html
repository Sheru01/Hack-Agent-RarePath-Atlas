How it works, in one minute

From a scattered search to one clear question.

  1. The problem

    A rare diagnosis arrives. The research sits in scattered papers, databases, and patient groups. No one can see the whole picture.

  2. The atlas

    RarePath draws the map. Every connection comes from a public source, with the section and the date we read it.

  3. The limit

    Every connection also says what it does not prove. A shared gene or region is not a shared treatment.

  4. The 10× idea

    The goal is to reach an expert's go or no-go decision faster. We think the atlas compresses the search and framing work. That is a hypothesis to test, not a result.

  5. Why this way

    Evidence first: the sources decide what enters the map. AI second: it only drafts questions. Human judgment always: a person reviews every step.
